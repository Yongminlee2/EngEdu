병아리와 함께 영어를 배웁니다. 알파벳 따라쓰기부터 실전 회화까지 한 앱에 이어져 있고, 문제마다 그림이 붙어 있어 글을 다 읽지 못해도 짚어 갈 수 있습니다.

■ 알파벳부터 실전까지 한 흐름

듣기 · 말하기 · 쓰기 · 문법 · 독해 · 어휘를 나눠서 연습하고, 영역마다 레벨이 오릅니다. 홈 화면이 어느 영역이 약한지 짚어 줍니다.

## 영어(미국) (en)

**앱 이름** (29자)
```
Peep English - ABC to Talking
```
**간단한 설명** (77자)
```
From tracing the alphabet to real conversation. Every question has a picture.
```
**자세한 설명**
```
Learn English with a chick. Everything from tracing the alphabet to real conversation lives in one app, and every question carries a picture — so you can follow along before you can read every word.

■ One path from the first letter to real conversation
· Alphabet tracing — draw A to Z stroke by stroke
· Elementary English — phonics, picture words, first sentences
· Middle and high school — grade-by-grade basics, grammar, reading
· Practical English — travel and daily conversation, exam-style passages

■ Practise each skill on its own
Listening, speaking, writing, grammar, reading and vocabulary each have their own level. The home screen points out which one is weakest.

■ Speaking out loud
Say the sentence into the microphone and see how close you got. Listening questions let you slow the speech down and hear it again.

■ A playground for your fingers
Pop balloons, draw lines to match — learn words by touching them.

■ Allowance wallet
Correct answers earn allowance. It can be exchanged for real pocket money by agreement, and a PIN keeps it from being cashed out by accident. Spend what you keep on hearts, hints and stickers.

■ 14 languages
Change the app's language in Settings — no need to change the whole phone.

■ Works without internet
Progress is stored on the device only. Nearly everything works in airplane mode.
```
